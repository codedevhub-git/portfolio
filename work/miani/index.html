Míani: The World Within

A living 3D world grown from one person’s memories, for their family to explore together.

Want something like this?
  • Godot + Node + Pythongame, world server, local AI
  • LANshared world with chat and encryption
  • Localspeech-to-text and document reading
  • 2installers: family and host

The project

Families add memories (stories, photos, voice) in a private “keeper’s room”, and Míani turns them into a world of lands, seas, towns and creatures that the family explores and discovers together. It runs entirely at home, with nothing sent to the cloud.

Memory vault

A keeper app to add chapters, people and memories, which become places in the world.

Living world

Weather, wildlife, seas with boats and whales, towns, flowers and day and night.

Play together

Family members join the same world over the home network, with emotes, name tags and chat.

Private by design

Local AI for transcription and reading documents, with nothing uploaded.

Under the hood

Built with

  • Godot 4.7
  • Node.js + TypeScript
  • SQLite
  • Python (local AI)
  • Electron admin

Míani grew out of Legacy Viewer, an earlier Electron app of mine where a family’s photos, videos and stories became planets in a 3D galaxy you could fly through (v2.3, Windows installer). The pictures show a demo world built from made-up sample memories. The Windows build uses a Godot engine I compiled from source with encrypted game data.
