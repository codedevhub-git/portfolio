The project

ShopTrack is a desktop product built to sell. Each copy is locked to one PC by a hardware-bound license with tiers (demo, standard, complete), and custom builds come from a single command. Inside are a shop’s customers, vehicles, jobs, parts, inventory with barcode scanning, receipts, financial reports and one-click backups, in Spanish and English. Phones on the shop’s Wi-Fi can open it too.

Customers & vehicles

Full history per customer and per vehicle, searchable from anywhere.

Jobs & parts

Work orders with parts, labor, media and printable receipts.

Inventory

Barcode scanning, locations, suppliers and low-stock alerts.

Licensing

Keys tied to the machine’s hardware ID, tier-based features and a demo build for prospects.

Backups

Zip backup and restore of the database and photos in one click.

Shop Wi-Fi access

Mechanics can open it on their phones over the local network.
