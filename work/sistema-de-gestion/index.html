Sistema de Gestión

An offline business system for a construction-supply store: inventory, invoices, accounting and forecasts.

The project

A Windows desktop app that runs the back office of a building-materials business without needing the internet or a monthly subscription. Inventory with stock alerts and margins, invoices and receivables, expenses, suppliers, reports and a prediction screen for what to reorder.

Inventory

Materials with cost, sale price, margin, supplier and stock status. Low-stock alerts on the dashboard.

Invoices & receivables

Paid, pending and overdue invoices, with payment method and totals owed.

Accounting

Income, expenses and cash flow by category and period.

Predictions

Inventory turnover, demand forecasts and ABC analysis to decide what to reorder.

Reports & export

PDF and Excel reports, plus automatic backups.

Under the hood

Built with

  • Electron
  • Express
  • SQLite (sql.js)
  • EJS
  • PDFKit
  • Excel export

Electron wraps a local Express server, with SQLite stored in a single file the owner can back up. electron-builder makes a Windows installer. These screens show seeded demo data, not the client’s records.
