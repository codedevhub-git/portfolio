Before you reach out


How much does a website cost?
Most small-business sites start around $800, and sites with a lead system start around $1,200. Custom tools and platforms are quoted. You get a fixed price in writing before anything starts.
How long does it take?
A focused business site usually launches in about a week once I have your info and photos. Apps and software are planned in phases, and you see working pieces early.
Do I have to pay monthly?
No subscription is required for a custom build, and you own the code. Hosting can cost nearly nothing on modern static hosting. Bigger apps may need a database or server, and I’ll tell you exactly what that costs before we start.
Do you work in Spanish?
Sí. I work with clients in English or Spanish, and nearly every site I build is bilingual.
