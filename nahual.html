NAHUAL

NAHUAL is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own Windows PC. It is created and used by Edgar A. Robledo (NopalCoder Creation).

What it does with Google

If you choose to connect a Google account, NAHUAL can read and send your email, manage your calendar, look up your contacts, keep your tasks, and work with your Drive, Docs and Sheets, only when you ask it to.

The AI runs on your own computer. Your Google data is not sent to any NopalCoder server.
