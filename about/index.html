About

I’m Edgar. I build things that work.

I’m an independent software developer in Lancaster, South Carolina. CodeDevHub is me: no agency, no outsourcing. When you hire me, you work with the person writing your code.

I started with websites for local businesses, and the work kept growing into the tools behind them: the admin panels, the lead inboxes, the POS, the invoices. Today I run my own SaaS platform for Latino small businesses (Nanarii), build bilingual sites for contractors, clinics, restaurants and nonprofits, and write desktop software that stores run on.

On my own time I build games and engines under the name NopalCoding, plus a private AI that lives on my PC. That’s where I learn the hard things: real-time 3D, performance, testing at scale. Then I bring them back to client work.

I work in English and Spanish, I reply fast, and I give you a fixed price before I start.
