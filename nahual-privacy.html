NAHUAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

NAHUAL is a personal AI assistant that runs on the user's own Windows PC. It is made by NopalCoder Creation (Edgar A. Robledo).

What NAHUAL can access

If you connect a Google account, NAHUAL can access the Google data you allow on Google's consent screen: Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Tasks, Drive, Docs and Sheets. It uses that access to do what you ask.

Where your data goes

NAHUAL runs on your computer. Google data it reads stays on your computer, in NAHUAL's own files. NopalCoder Creation has no server that receives it, and does not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

Your Google sign-in is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC.

Google API Services

NAHUAL's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can disconnect a Google account in NAHUAL's Settings, delete NAHUAL's data from its Settings, or remove its access at any time from your Google Account's security page.
