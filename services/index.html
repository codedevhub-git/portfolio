FAQ

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need — a simple site costs a lot less than a custom tool or platform. Text me what you’re building and I’ll give you a real number for your project, with a fixed price in writing before anything starts.

How long does it take?

A focused business site usually launches in about a week once I have your info and photos. Apps and software are planned in phases, and you see working pieces early.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No subscription is required for a custom build, and you own the code. Hosting can cost nearly nothing on modern static hosting. Bigger apps may need a database or server, and I’ll tell you exactly what that costs before we start.

Do you work in Spanish?

Sí. I work with clients in English or Spanish, and nearly every site I build is bilingual.

Can you fix my existing website?

Yes. I can improve it or rebuild it, keep your domain and email, and protect the search rankings you already have.

Do you only work locally?

I’m in Lancaster, SC and happy to meet around Lancaster, Rock Hill and Charlotte, but I already build for clients in California and Mexico, too.

Is it just you?

Yes. You work directly with me, the person writing your code, from start to finish. No account managers, no outsourcing.
